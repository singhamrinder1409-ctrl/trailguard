# TrailGuard

A hiking-navigation design prototype focused on clearer directions, junction planning and preparation before a hike.

**Design portfolio · SENG 310 · University of Victoria · Spring 2026**

## View the project

[Download the final presentation](docs/TrailGuard_Final_Presentation.pptx). Open it in PowerPoint or another compatible viewer. This repository contains the project presentation and design write-up; an executable navigation application is not included.

## Design

- **One-Glance Navigation:** quick directions intended to reduce repeated phone checking.
- **Smart Junction Planner:** guidance around trail junctions.
- **Pre-Hike Checklist:** preparation reminders before setting out.

The team moved from paper sketches to a medium-fidelity prototype. Pilot feedback led to multiple-trail support, a progress bar and interface fixes.

## Evaluation and findings

Eight participants completed trail selection, checklist and navigation tasks in moderated, think-aloud testing. The presentation reports that trail selection was easy, the checklist was useful and One-Glance mode reduced cognitive load. Junction clarity and error recovery remained concerns. These are reported usability findings, not quantified safety or navigation-performance claims.

## What I would improve

Improve junction guidance and recovery after a wrong turn, then evaluate the revised tasks. Offline support, elevation information and emergency contacts were future-work proposals rather than demonstrated features.
